title: Automations
---

- **Automations** - Give an agent a prompt and a schedule, and it runs on its own. Results land in a channel you pick or in your DM. Pause, edit, or hit **Run now** anytime.
- **Starters** - Not sure where to begin? Pick a suggestion like Morning briefing, Topic watch, or Daily learning drill and tweak the prompt to taste.
- **Each automation has its own page** - the prompt, the schedule, where it posts, and a history of recent runs with what went wrong when one fails.
- **Status that tells the truth** - a new automation shows "Applying…" until the agent actually picks it up, and failing runs are marked in red instead of hiding.
- **A new Agents page** - your agents are now a binder of collectible cards. Tap one to open its profile.
