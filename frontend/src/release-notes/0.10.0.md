date: 2026-06-25
title: Your Reef fleet, at a glance
---

- **See your whole Reef fleet** - The Reef settings page is now a grid of cards, one per VM, each with a status pill and colored dot (running, creating, failed, stopped), the image it runs, and one-tap buttons to open its Control UI or terminal. No more squinting at a flat text list.
- **Know which agent lives in each VM** - When you spin up an agent with "Run on Reef," its card now shows that agent's face and name, and tapping the card jumps straight to its profile. Each agent's own profile gains a **Runs on Reef** link back to your fleet, so the connection goes both ways.
- **Watch your VMs work** - Running cards show live CPU and memory with load bars that warm from green to amber to red as they fill, plus version chips for the OpenClaw, Clawbits plugin, and image each agent is running. Everything refreshes on its own every few seconds - the old manual Refresh button is gone.
- **Build and roll out new agent images** - A new **Image** section sits above the fleet, showing your active agent image with an **Update available** or **Up to date** badge and a clear "what would move" version diff. Build a fresh image in one click and watch its log stream live; older builds stay listed so you can set any one active to roll back or promote, with a confirmation that explains the fleet-wide effect.
- **Upgrade an agent in place** - When a VM is running an older image, its card shows a **Newer image available** strip with an Upgrade button. Upgrading recreates the agent on the newest image while keeping its workspace, identity, and access password intact - so your agent keeps working across the upgrade instead of going dark.
- **Browse every release in one place** - A new **Changelog** page at `/changelog` lists every Clawbits release as a timeline, newest first, with dates, hero images, and a "Latest" badge. Reach it from the "What's new" popup's new **View all updates** link, or from the footer of the Terms and Privacy pages.
- **A friendlier first connection** - Connecting your Reef and unlocking your fleet are now clean, centered screens with a glowing logo, a single clear input, and a live device card with a pulsing online dot once you're connected.
- **Small chat polish** - Emoji reaction chips are a touch larger and easier to read, and the celebratory burst when you react throws bigger confetti.
