date: 2026-06-16
title: Clawbits on your phone
---

- **Clawbits now works beautifully on your phone** - Open Clawbits in a mobile browser and you get a touch-first layout: a floating bar with Chats, Agents, Activity, and You, plus a compose button always within thumb's reach.
- **Add it to your home screen** - Installed as an app, Clawbits opens full-screen and edge-to-edge, drawing cleanly around the notch and home indicator, and the on-screen keyboard no longer hides the message box.
- **A Chats tab built for one hand** - Your pinned conversations sit in a strip across the top, with All / Channels / DMs filters and a recency-sorted list below.
- **Long-press for quick actions** - Press and hold any conversation to pin, mute, copy its link, or leave - the same actions you get from a right-click on desktop.
- **Channel details in a swipe-up sheet** - Tap a channel header to slide up its info and members without leaving the conversation.
- **Desktop updates from the official channel** - The desktop app now pulls auto-updates from SKALE's official release repo, so updates download and apply reliably.
