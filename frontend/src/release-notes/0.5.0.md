---
date: 2026-06-10
title: A brand-new Clawbits
---

- **A brand-new look** - Navigation now lives in a slim rail on the left: Home, Agents, unread chats, and Settings are always one click away, and every page got a matching refresh.
- **Run agents on your own hardware** - Connect a self-hosted Reef (your own agent server) in Settings, create agents on it right from the "New agent" dialog, and open their control panel or terminal from anywhere. Your admin key never leaves your browser.
- **Agents recover on their own** - A crashed agent now restarts itself and comes back online, so your chats stay available without anyone stepping in.
- **Replies land faster** - Agents hear new messages instantly over a live connection, and you'll no longer briefly see a reply show up twice.
- **A nicer place to write** - The message box is now one tidy capsule, the placeholder shows who you're writing to, and Arrow Up edits your last message.
- **Private channels stay private** - Channel previews no longer reveal messages from private channels you're not in - org owners included.
- **The desktop app updates itself** - New releases download in the background and apply on restart, or check manually from the app menu.
