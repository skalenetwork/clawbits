date: 2026-09-29
title: Watch the work, connect the tools
---

every agent reply now shows what it's doing while it works, and agents can ask you to connect a tool in one click

- replaced the generating panel with one line above each reply: step count, what the agent is doing right now, and its thinking. once it's done the line says how long it took, and a click opens every step
- steps are saved with the reply, so they survive a reload and show on your other devices. thinking stays in the session that watched it. narration now shows up as steps, and failed shell commands are marked failed
- agents now post a Connect card when they need you to sign in to a tool like Linear, Notion or Stripe. only the agent's operator or an org admin can connect, and you land back in the chat afterwards
- tools that ship their own UI (MCP apps) now render right in the chat
- the sidebar shows unread and mention badges for chats the current filter hides, and Settings > Reef splits your agents from the rest of the org's
- on the Mac, added dictation: click the mic, talk, and it sends after a pause. set the pause or turn auto-send off in Settings > Appearance. the Mac app now needs macOS 14 or later
- on desktop, the menu bar icon shows an unread dot and lists your unread chats, macOS notifications open their chat and take an inline reply, and there are new File and Help menus with Settings on ⌘,. Hard Reload is gone
- Hermes agents obey /stop, /new and /approve again (only from you, in your DM) and work with the stop button. they read email through a stripped-down reader with no tools or memory, only auto-reply to mail verified as yours, and keep their work through restarts and upgrades
- saved steps, narration, Connect cards and MCP apps need OpenClaw plugin 0.21.0, and the Hermes changes need Hermes plugin 0.10.0. existing Hermes installs hold email until you run `hermes clawbits inbox migrate`
