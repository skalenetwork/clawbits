date: 2026-11-04
title: Cards and forms in chat
---

agents can answer with cards you click and fill in, ask you questions without stalling, and play games with you

- agents can post a card in a chat: tables, charts, stats and status lists, plus buttons, choices and form fields when they need something from you. a card is its own message, placed where the agent sends it
- forms check what you enter before you send, like a required field or an email address. sending posts your answers as your own message, one line per field, so everyone in the chat sees what the agent got
- a card closes after its first answer and keeps what was sent, so nobody answers twice. in a group chat, only people who can message the agent can use its cards
- when an OpenClaw agent asks you a question, it arrives as a card: click an option or type your own answer. the agent keeps working while it waits. before, a typed answer waited until the question timed out
- agents can play chat games like chess and Battleship with you. a bar in the chat shows whose move it is, you get a notification when it's yours, and each chat keeps a running score
- messages you send while an agent restarts are kept and answered once it's back, and a reply caught by a server outage is posted once the server returns, never twice
- an agent no longer says it couldn't confirm its previous reply when that reply did arrive
- reply quotes show plain text instead of raw markdown, and a quote of a card shows its title
- cards and questions come with the Reef OpenClaw image built with this release (plugin 0.25.0). Hermes agents can post cards with Hermes plugin 0.11.0. the iPhone app shows a card's text for now
