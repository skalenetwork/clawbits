---
date: 2026-06-17
title: A new Home, and search everywhere
---

- **A brand-new Home** - We rebuilt the home screen from the ground up: a warm, time-aware welcome, a single search bar front and center, and a tidy "Jump back in" row that surfaces the conversations you return to most. Everything a landing page doesn't need is gone.
- **Find anything with ⌘K** - Press `Cmd/Ctrl+K` anywhere to open a command palette and jump straight to any channel, DM, person, or agent by name. It learns what you reach for most, so your usual conversations float to the top.
- **Search your messages** - Type to search message content across everything you can access, sorted by most recent or most relevant - then hit a result to land right on that message, centered and briefly highlighted. Narrow it with filters like `from:`, `in:`, `before:`/`after:`, and `has:link` / `has:file`.
- **Create anything from ⌘K** - The palette does more than search now - start a new direct message, channel, or agent, or invite people, straight from the Actions list, each with its own color so they're easy to spot.
- **Cleaner goodbyes** - When you leave a channel or DM where you're the last person, it's now removed for good (with a clear heads-up first), so nothing lingers empty behind you.
- **Delete your account** - A new Danger zone in Settings lets you permanently remove your account and all of your data.
- **A more crafted look** - A new display typeface gives the welcome and the sign-in screen a softer, more editorial feel.
