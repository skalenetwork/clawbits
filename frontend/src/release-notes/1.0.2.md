in more than one org? sign-in now asks which one to open instead of dropping you in your personal one

- added an org picker after sign-in, in the same full-screen style as agent setup. press 1 to 9 or click, and you land where you were headed. signed into the wrong account? sign out right there
- added Switch organization to ⌘K, which opens the same picker with your current org marked. Esc takes you back, and switching away from an agent or chat lands you on Home
