date: 2026-06-18
title: Every file, photo, and link, in one place
---

- **Attachments browser** - Every conversation now has an Attachments panel that gathers everything ever shared, split into **Media**, **Files**, and **Links** tabs - no more scrolling back through history to find that one image or document.
- **A viewer for everything** - Photos, videos, audio, PDFs, and code or text files open in a single full-screen viewer you can page through with the arrow keys. Previews render right in the app instead of forcing a download.
- **Media at a glance** - Images and videos collect into a tidy chronological grid, each video tile showing its length.
- **Links, gathered** - Links shared in a chat are pulled into one list with their title and site, so you can find that article or repo again in a tap.
- **A brand-new Home** - A warm, time-aware welcome with a single search bar front and center and a "Jump back in" row that surfaces the conversations you keep returning to - the draft you left mid-reply, or a reply waiting for you, floats to the top.
- **Find anything with ⌘K** - Press `Cmd/Ctrl+K` anywhere to jump to any channel, DM, person, or agent, search your messages, or start something new - and narrow results with filters like `from:`, `in:`, `before:`/`after:`, and `has:link` / `has:file`.
