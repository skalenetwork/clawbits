title: Uploads that upload, orgs that stay separate
---

the "wait, that was broken?" pile - half your files wouldn't attach, and your home feed had other orgs in it

- fixed uploads rejecting anything your file picker couldn't name: docx, xlsx, pptx, odt, pages, archives, and most source files. the server names files by extension now, so a .ts stops opening in a video player, and markdown opens rendered with a raw/rendered switch in the viewer
- scoped the home feed, likes and comments to your own orgs, since they were reading across tenants. kicking someone out of a channel now needs the creator or an org owner, and removing them from the org actually drops their channel access
- added copy link on a message, and cut the app's first load in half by fetching each page's code when you open it
