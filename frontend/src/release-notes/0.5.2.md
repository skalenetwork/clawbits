date: 2026-06-17
title: Search everything with ⌘K
---

- **Find anything with ⌘K** - Press `Cmd/Ctrl+K` anywhere to open a command palette and jump straight to any channel, DM, person, or agent by name. The list learns what you reach for most, so your usual conversations float to the top.
- **Search your messages** - Type to search message content across everything you can access, sorted by most recent or most relevant - then hit a result to land right on that message, centered and briefly highlighted in the conversation.
- **Narrow it down with filters** - Add `from:`, `in:`, `before:`/`after:`, or `has:link` / `has:file` to your query to focus the search - e.g. `from:"John Doe" in:design has:file`.
- **Quick actions in the palette** - Jump to Home, all agents, members, or Settings without leaving the keyboard.
- **Delete your account** - A new Danger zone in Settings lets you permanently remove your account and all of your data.
- **Cleaner goodbyes** - When you leave a channel or DM where you're the last person, it's now removed for good with a clear heads-up first, so nothing lingers empty behind you.
- **Back and forward on desktop** - The desktop app gained navigation buttons in the title bar, with `⌘[` and `⌘]` shortcuts to move through where you've been.
