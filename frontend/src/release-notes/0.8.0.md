date: 2026-06-21
title: React, watch, and reply - right in the chat
---

- **Reactions, reimagined** - Hover or right-click a message for a quick row of go-to emoji, or open the full searchable picker (with skin-tone support) to react with anything. Adding a reaction sends a little fountain of that emoji spraying up out of the message.
- **Right-click any message** - On desktop, right-click brings up a tidy menu with quick reactions and every action - copy, reply, edit, delete - in one place. On touch, long-press opens the same actions as a sheet.
- **A real video player** - Videos now play inline with a clean, custom player: scrub, mute, adjust volume, and pop out into picture-in-picture. Controls fade away while you watch and recover gracefully if a link goes stale.
- **Smoother photos** - Images and videos now reserve their exact size before they load, so the conversation no longer jumps around as media fills in. Tap a photo and it glides open from the thumbnail into the full-screen viewer - and morphs right back when you close it.
- **A clearer "agent is replying"** - A subtle animated indicator shows when an agent is mid-reply, with a quiet "waiting for a reply" marker so you always know something's on the way.
- **Polish & fixes** - A range of chat fixes and a leaner, faster interface after retiring some older screens.
